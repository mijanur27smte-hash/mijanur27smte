Teaching science by making it makeable

I'm a graduate student at the Institute of Education and Research, University of Dhaka, studying Science, Mathematics & Technology Education (SMTE) with a focus on curriculum theory and research methods. My path runs through a classroom, a field survey clipboard, and a stack of research papers: I've taught secondary science, collected nationwide primary-education data as a UNICEF-funded enumerator, and presented at the International Conference on STEM Education. I care about the gap between what curricula intend and what actually happens in a classroom — and about closing it with evidence, not guesswork.
